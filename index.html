Where do you want to start?

Six honest routes. Pick the one that sounds like you, open it, and it will tell you what to try first and where to go. None of them promise you anything.

I want to understand what this even isStart with the plain-language version, no product in sight

Brainwave entrainment is a claim about rhythm. Put a steady pulse in your ears and the brain tends to fall in step with it. That much is a well-known effect. What that is worth to you personally is a different question, and it is the one worth asking slowly.

The guide explains the frequency bands, what they are associated with, and how to tell a thoughtfully made program from a page that is only selling you something.

What to try first: read the guide end to end before you spend anything. It is the cheapest thing on this site.

I want to sleepThe deepest part of the catalogue, and the longest track record

Sleep is where this catalogue is deepest. The Delta Sleep System has been around for decades, was carried on national television for two years, and is still the one people come back to. If you are going to start anywhere with audio, most people start here.

It is an audio program. You listen. Nothing is worn, nothing is applied, and you can stop whenever you like.

What to try first: one program for two weeks, at the same time each night. Change nothing else that week, so you actually learn what you are looking at.

I want to focus, or think more clearlyThe other half of the catalogue, and the one people underrate

The calm-alert end is where a lot of people get more out of this than they expect. Alpha sits in the settled, awake, not-trying-too-hard zone. Gamma is the sharper one, and it has become the most talked about band in the last few years.

These are listening programs too. Headphones make a difference for some of them, so read the notes on each one rather than assuming.

What to try first: one focused session in the morning, before the day starts pulling at you. Five days. Then notice whether the difference is real, or whether you just like having a ritual.

I want to feel it in my body, not just hear itVibroacoustic: sound you sit inside

Vibroacoustic tools put transducers into a chair, table or lounger so low frequency sound moves through you instead of past you. People describe it as being inside the music rather than listening to it. That description is the honest one, because the research does not get much more specific than that yet.

You can try this without buying anything. A session with a practitioner is how most people find out whether it is for them, and it costs a great deal less than owning the hardware.

What to try first: book one session somewhere near you. Do not buy a chair until you have felt one.
Compare the equipment and the options →

Worth knowing: the FDA classifies vibroacoustic devices as therapeutic vibrators, not medical devices. Anyone selling you one as medical treatment is overstating it.

I want the personalised versionBio-Tuning: your own nervous system, measured

Bio-Tuning measures your heart rate variability to find your own baseline frequency, then builds tracks around it instead of handing you a generic file. It is the deepest end of everything on this site, and it is a practitioner route rather than a shopping route.

One honest note about the cost: the practitioner system runs into five figures. If you want the experience rather than the equipment, a session with a certified practitioner costs a great deal less.

What to try first: a single session with someone certified. Then decide whether the system is worth anything to you.

I want to build a practice around itTraining, certification, and what it commits you to

There is a real certification path here, run through the Center for Neuroacoustic Research, with continuing membership and a directory listing. It is a serious commitment, not a weekend course.

Two things worth reading before you commit. The certification terms require you not to make claims about outcomes, and they require you to consult the Center before using their material on a website. Both are reasonable, and both are worth knowing on day one rather than month six.

What to try first: read the terms and the levels of practice, then talk to a working practitioner about what the business actually looks like.

What we mean by sound state

Different sounds can support different states. People use sound to relax, focus, settle, or create a quieter routine. The goal is not to promise miracles. The goal is to help you understand what these tools are and how to use them well.

The deeper goal is even simpler: learn how to move between states intentionally, safely, and without giving up the wheel to whatever is trying to pull at your attention.
