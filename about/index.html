About

About Sound State Guide

Sound State Guide is a practical site about sound-based tools, brainwave entrainment, and the Dr. Jeffrey Thompson lineage.

We’re here to make the topic easier to understand without turning it into hype.

A lot of content in this space is either too technical, too mystical, or too salesy. We want to do something simpler: explain what these tools are, how people use them, and what a reasonable person should expect.

Who runs this site

Sound State Guide is written and maintained by Chris Bruyette. He is not a clinician, a researcher, or a representative of Dr. Jeffrey Thompson's organization, and nothing here should be read as if he were.

He keeps this site because the choice in this space is usually between unreadable and unbelievable, and because there should be somewhere that says plainly what is known, what is uncertain, and what is simply marketing.

What we believe

  • clear
  • honest
  • practical
  • human
  • useful

We believe people deserve a plain-language explanation before they decide whether a sound-based tool is worth their time or money.

We also believe the strongest claims should be reserved for the strongest evidence.

What this site is for

Sound State Guide is here to help people understand brainwave entrainment, learn the basic terms, compare sound-based tools, see how the Dr. Jeffrey Thompson lineage fits together, and make a choice based on clarity, not pressure.

If you’re new, this site should give you a calm place to start. If you already know the basics, it should help you compare the options without getting lost.

What this site is not

  • medical promises
  • miracle claims
  • one-size-fits-all sales language
  • a cult of personality
  • a replacement for medical care

We respect the topic too much to dress it up.

Our editorial stance

We try to keep every page readable, grounded, easy to follow, free of unnecessary jargon, careful about evidence, and honest about limits.

If something is mixed, we say it is mixed. If something is uncertain, we say it is uncertain. If something seems useful but limited, we say that too.

That is the standard.

Who this is for

Sound State Guide is for people who want to understand sound-based wellness without the fluff, learn how the tools fit together, compare products before buying, start simple, and make sense of the Thompson ecosystem.

This site is meant to be a guide, not a sales pitch.

How we handle recommendations

If we recommend a tool or program, it will be because we think it fits a real goal.

That means we will look at what the tool is for, how it is used, whether it is beginner-friendly, whether it seems overcomplicated, and whether it matches the reader’s needs.

We want the recommendation to make sense, not just sound good.

A note on the lineage

A large part of this space traces back to Dr. Jeffrey Thompson and the Center for Neuroacoustic Research.

That lineage matters because it helps separate direct source material, later product layers, community or training layers, what is well established, and what is still evolving.

We think that context helps readers make better decisions.

What this site adds

Beyond explanation, this site also tries to help people learn how to shift on purpose. That means a manual-shift mindset, not passive drift.

Sound should support attention, not steal it. The goal is to build the kind of mental control that lets you move in and out of state deliberately.

That is why cognitive security matters here: keep your hand on the wheel, learn your gears, and use tools that help you stay the chooser.

If you’re just getting started

Start with the guide. If you want the background, read the Thompson lineage page next. If you want to compare tools, go to the tools page.

That gives you a clean path through the site without having to guess where to begin.

Contact / future note

As this site grows, we’ll add more practical pages, comparison notes, and reader-friendly guides.

For now, the goal is simple: help you understand the space well enough to choose wisely.
