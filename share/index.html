Experiences, not claims

What did you actually notice?

Almost nothing in this field is written by people who tried it and said what happened. It is written by people selling something. We would like to collect the first kind.

What we publish

Your words, about your own experience, with your name if you want it there and without it if you do not. What you tried, roughly how long, and what you noticed.

We do not edit the meaning. If you say it did nothing, we publish that.

What we will not publish

  • anything you have not given us permission to publish
  • claims about what sound does to other people
  • anything presented as medical treatment
  • testimonials dressed up as evidence

If your experience is unusual, that is interesting. It is still one person's experience, and we will present it that way.

Add yours

Tell us what happened

The useful version of this is specific and unflattering. What you tried, how long you gave it, and what you actually noticed. If the answer is nothing, that is a real answer and it belongs here.

If you would rather not use a form, there is a contact route on the About page.

What happens after you send it

It lands in an inbox, not a database. Someone reads it. If you asked us to publish it, we will come back to you before anything goes up, and you can change your mind at that point without explaining why.

If you did not ask us to publish it, it stays private and it still helps, because it tells us what people are actually running into.

Why we are asking at all

Because the alternative in this field is a wall of promises written by people with something to sell, and that is not useful to anyone deciding whether to try something.

An experience written by one person is not proof of anything. A hundred of them, read honestly, start to be worth something. That is the whole idea.
